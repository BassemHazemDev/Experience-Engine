# Final EICS 2027 Verification

Checked: 2026-09-17 against the current official EICS 2027 Full Papers & Technical Notes call.

Confirmed by the official EICS page:
- Full Papers and Technical Notes are accepted.
- Technical Notes focus on shorter, focused system/technical contributions.
- A Technical Note requires an illustrative example of the system.
- Validation may use simulation, feasibility, or comparisons; a user study is not mandatory.
- There is no stated page-length restriction for Full Papers or Technical Notes.
- Submissions follow ACM TAPS and are made through the Precision Conference System.
- Review is double-blind.
- EICS 2027 publishes through PACM-EICS and is under ACM Open.

The official call currently says there are three submission deadlines per year but the
retrieved page does not expose the concrete dates for the active 2027 round. This package
therefore deliberately does not invent a deadline.

The actual PCS round, author metadata, conflicts, and final TAPS/PCS upload checks remain
human submission steps.
